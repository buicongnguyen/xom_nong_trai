🌾 Xóm Nông Trại · Browser demo

Your farm stays on this device

This is a single-player demo. It has no online accounts or cloud saves.

What the browser saves

Your farm progress, inventory, game preferences, and a local sign-in hint are stored in this browser. The demo uses that information to restore your game when you return.

Where the data goes

The website downloads the game files needed to play. Farm actions run on your device, and your saved farm is not sent to the original game's servers.

Keeping or removing your farm

Progress belongs to the browser and device you use. Clearing site data or switching browsers may leave you with a new farm. To reset this demo's farm, use Reset farm.
